Part 1 · Why our own platform

Our box. Our data. Our rules.

Vercel is fine for prototypes. Team tools touch real company and client data — so they run on a server we own, with Dokploy, an open-source “press deploy, get a URL” platform, on top.

Reason 01

Own the data.

Your data stays on our server, under our rules — never on someone else's cloud.

Reason 02

Secure everything.

Every app sits behind the company sign-in before a single request reaches it.

Reason 03

Control all the things.

Server, databases, access — ours to inspect and change. No support tickets. We go look.

One hard boundary Internal tools only. If a customer might ever touch it, it belongs on engineering's stack — talk to them first.

Part 3 · The front gate

The wall.

Every request to our apps is checked at the front gate — no company sign-in, no entry. Everything you ship today is automatically company-only. But the gate cuts two ways:

Humans

Sail through.

You sign in once in your browser; after that the gate is invisible. Your apps are private to the company without writing a line of security code.

Machines

Hit the wall.

Scripts, robots, other services — Slack included — can't sign in with Google. They get bounced, silently.

Two consequences:outside services can never call our apps — we check for news insteadClaude needs a pass to deploy — that's WARP →

🚀 Lab 3 · 25 min — ship it

Deploy it — by saying so.

  1. Tell Claude: “deploy this to dokploy” — the dokploy-deploy skill takes it from there.
  2. Claude will stop and ask where your database should live. Say: “create a new managed Postgres in my project.” That database is yours for good — every app you ship after today shares it.
  3. Then Claude builds your app, wires up its address, and checks it's healthy — you just watch.
what you'll see claude do
→ project-create # yours — the only one you see
→ postgres-create # YOURS, permanently
→ compose-create / compose-update
→ domain-create # https: false — no redirect loop
→ compose-deploy # …build minutes…
→ curl https://<your-app>/healthz # via WARP
✓ {"ok":true,"db":"up"} — through the wall
✓ Checkpoint

Deployment done, and Claude says the health check passed. Stuck more than 2 minutes? Wave — we'll hand you a spare and fix yours after.
